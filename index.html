(01) Introduction

I build systems that run, and then keep running.

Six years inside law-enforcement IT in Zimbabwe taught me what happens when a data system is wrong and someone acts on it anyway. That is still the thing I care about: not whether a model scores well on a slide, but whether the thing holds up in production, on a bad day, with real people depending on it.

I finished an MSc in Data Science at Akademia WSB in 2026, and I am working through a DevOps engineering track now. In between, I ship: a chess federation runs its tournaments on software I wrote; a prediction engine retrains and publishes itself twice a day without me touching it. Everything on this page is open, live, or both — you should not have to take my word for any of it.
