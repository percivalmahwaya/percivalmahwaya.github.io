(04) Research

MSc thesis: crime hotspots and patrol tiers.

Applying Machine Learning to Crime Hotspot Prediction: A Decision Support Model for Public Safety Resource Allocation. Akademia WSB, 2026. Awarded a distinction.

The model predicts which Chicago police beats are hotspots. The thesis then ranks beats by how stable that status is across five years, 2019 to 2023, and turns the ranking into three patrol tiers.

34.2%

of beats are hotspots in all five years. These are Tier 1, the persistent hotspots. 30.2% are Tier 2 (hotspot in one to four years). 35.6% are Tier 3 (never).
