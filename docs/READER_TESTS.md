# Reader tests (you run these; I cannot)

Use a person who is not you. Do not explain the site. Record answers verbatim. Pass marks are written first.

## A. Hiring manager (DevOps, data or platform)
5 seconds: show the homepage, hide it, ask "What does this person do? What would you email them about?"
Pass: says data/platform/DevOps engineer and names one system. 
30 seconds: ask "What is their strongest proof? What worries you?"
Pass: names test counts, CI or a live system as proof, and nobody calls the work fake or padded.
Watch for: "QA tester" read as the whole identity.

## B. PhD supervisor
5 seconds: "What research does this person do?" Pass: names crime hotspot prediction or the thesis.
30 seconds: "Would you take a call with them? What is missing?" Pass: finds the thesis result, the model comparison and the stability tiers without scrolling for them. Record what they ask for (publication, PDF, supervisor name).

## C. Peer builder
5 seconds: "What did they build?" Pass: names two of three projects.
30 seconds: open one case study. "What went wrong in that project?" Pass: they find the failure section in under 30 seconds and do not read it as a weakness.

## D. Screen-reader user (NVDA or VoiceOver)
Tasks: reach the main content, list the headings, find the contact email, read the status strip, read the FPL model comparison table.
Pass: skip link works, heading order has no gaps, table has a caption and row headers, dots are announced with their word.

## E. Colour-blind reader (or a deuteranopia simulator)
Tasks: tell which projects are live; find the lead model in the AUC chart; find the "what went wrong" row.
Pass: every answer is found without relying on colour.
